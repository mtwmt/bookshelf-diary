Frequently asked questions.

View all FAQs
Why can't some books be found after scanning?+−

Older, out-of-print, or niche books may not have public records and can be entered manually. When you add a volume with an ISBN to your library, the app attempts to contribute to the public catalog, depending on sync results.

Can I view my library without an internet connection?+−

Yes. Basic collection management works offline. Online features like ISBN lookup and Google Drive cloud backups require an internet connection.

How do I transfer my collection when switching phones?+−

You can export a local backup file and import it on your new phone. Free users who sign in can try backup/restore twice; Plus/Pro members enjoy unlimited local backups and restores, plus automated Google Drive backup. Backups must be restored manually and do not sync in real time; always confirm your backup before deleting the app or switching phones.

Who can I reach out to for suggestions or issues?+−

You can contact the developer through the Feedback page to report issues or suggest features. You can also check the Changelog.
