QWhy can't some books be found after scanning?

Older, out-of-print, or niche books may not have public records and can be entered manually. When you add a volume with an ISBN to your library, the app attempts to contribute to the public catalog, depending on sync results.

QWhat if AI Spine Scan misses or misidentifies a book?

Pro lets you take or pick a bookshelf photo to recognize spines and match candidate books, up to 100 times per month. Please confirm each book before adding to your library; for missed or mismatched books, use Title/Author search or re-scan the ISBN. Internet connection is required.

QCan I search and add a whole series at once?

With Pro, choose "Full Series" mode in "Add Manually" to search by title or author, select multiple volumes, and confirm editions and volume numbers before adding to your library. Results depend on catalogue sources; missing details can be filled in manually.

QHow do I enter volumes for omnibuses, box sets, or multi-part books?

When scanning, the app tries to detect the book format; please verify volume numbers and binding before adding. When adding manually, volume numbers don't have to be integers—you can enter formats like "1-3", "7+8+9", "13.5", or part labels.

QWhat if a scanned book is grouped into the wrong series?

Open the series, long-press the work you want to move, then tap "Move to..." at the bottom and pick the target series. The app groups books by title automatically; if it guesses wrong, you can adjust it on the confirmation screen before adding or move it afterward.

QHow can I efficiently manage a long series (50+ volumes)?

Use "Full Series" mode in "Add Manually" to create volumes 1 through N at once, then tap owned volumes on the work page to track progress and missing volumes.

QWhat can Book Calendar do?

Book Calendar is a Pro feature. Open "Calendar" from the top menu of "Library" to switch between two modes:

  • Releases: Lists new releases by publication date for your owned works, same series, and followed authors. Unseen releases show a notification count next to "Calendar" (excluding books already in your library or wishlist) and update total volume counts; add unowned books to Wishlist or owned ones straight to Library.
  • Purchases: Shows monthly purchase counts and recorded spending based on your purchase dates. Check "Purchased this month" for the full list and fill in "Price unrecorded" items. Records without a purchase date are excluded.

Release data comes from the public catalogue and may not cover every book on the market; internet is required. Free and Plus users tapping "Calendar" are directed to the subscription page.

QAre e-books supported?

Yes. When adding or editing a volume, select "E-book" as the edition and choose Kobo, BookWalker, Readmoo, Google Play, Pubu, or a custom platform. Home counts and spending separate Print and E-books. Barcode scanning defaults to Print; enter eISBN manually for e-books. E-books cannot be sold or lent.

QCan I log books borrowed from a library?

Yes. Tap "Records" in the top menu of "Library" to log books one by one with title, author, publisher, rating, reading status, notes, and cover. You can add them to Library or Wishlist later. Records is a Pro feature.

QHow do I track books lent to friends?

Tap "Loans" in the top menu of "Library" to record lent books (recipient, lent date, optional return-by date) and borrowed books. Lending does not change your owned count. Loans is a Pro feature. E-books cannot be lent.

QWhat can the Excel collection list do?

Don't want to scan books one by one? Fill out our Excel template on your computer and import it into the app, or reformat exports from another app to import directly (ISBNs will attempt to load covers; add missing covers manually). You can also export your library as a spreadsheet to review or share.

Import and export require sign-in; Free users get 2 total trials, while Plus and Pro are unlimited.

Migrating from another app? Try the Converter or download the Excel template. Exports (.xlsx or .csv) from BookBuddy can also be imported directly in the latest app under "Settings → Collection List (.xlsx) → Other formats".

QWhat's the difference between a local backup file and an Excel list?

They serve different purposes: use a backup file for full restoration, and Excel as an editable book list.

  • Local Backup File (.bookshelf): Includes your library data and local covers—ideal for restoring after switching phones or reinstalling. Sign-in and purchase status still need to be restored separately.
  • Excel Collection List: A human-readable spreadsheet for organizing, sharing, or migrating from other apps. Re-importing Excel cannot restore everything in a backup file. ISBNs will attempt to load covers, though missing ones must be added manually.

Rule of thumb: use a backup file to save your data, and Excel to edit or share.

QWhere is my data stored? Could it be lost?

Your personal library is stored locally on your device, and core features work without signing in. If Google Drive Backup is enabled, backup files are saved to your Google Drive; catalogue lookups and corrections connect to our service. Always make sure you have a working backup before switching devices or deleting the app.

QCan I sync across multiple devices?

There is no real-time sync; each device maintains its own library. Plus and Pro users can enable Google Drive Backup, then sign in and restore on a new phone.

QHow do I restore paid features after switching phones or reinstalling?

Sign in with the Gmail (Google account) you originally registered with so the app can verify your status. If your plan still doesn't show, use the original store account and tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings. Your library data still needs to be restored from a backup.

QHow do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Cancel through the subscription settings of your original app store. On iPhone/iPad, go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions and select Bookshelf Diary; on Android, open Google Play → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

After cancelling auto-renewal, your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period; free trial end times follow what is shown in the store.
