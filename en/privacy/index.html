Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This policy explains how Bookshelf Diary handles your personal information.

Data Storage & Ownership

Your personal library, purchase, and sale records are stored primarily on your local device, allowing offline browsing and organizing. Deleting the app may remove local data; please verify your backups before changing devices or reinstalling. Personal libraries do not sync across devices in real time. If you use Google Drive backups, backup files and book covers are stored in your connected Google Drive.

Sign-in & Purchases

When signing in with Google or Apple, we process platform-provided verified account IDs, email addresses, display names, and profile pictures for authentication, account linking, and membership management. Purchases are processed by the App Store / Google Play, and RevenueCat assists with validating transactions and subscription entitlements. Our backend stores membership status, expiration dates, purchase validation results, and feature usage to support restoring purchases, entitlement checks, and usage limits.

Permissions Used

  • Camera & Photos: Used for ISBN barcode scanning, spine recognition, and photos you take or select for book covers, resale listings, or feedback attachments.
  • File Access: Used to read and write covers and photos, back up and restore data, and import/export Excel files.
  • Google Drive: Used to create, manage, and restore app backups after you connect your account.

Third-Party Services

Book lookups transmit necessary ISBN, title, or author queries to catalog services and external sources (such as Google Books and Open Library) to retrieve book info and covers. Public book records and covers that you contribute or submit corrections for are sent to our services separately from your private library backups.

The free tier may display Google AdMob advertisements. Ad services may process device identifiers, network information, and interaction/diagnostic data for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention, subject to SDK and system settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.

AI Spine Recognition

When you use AI spine recognition, shelf photos you choose or capture are transmitted through our service to Google Gemini for recognition, returning spine text and candidate books. Please avoid uploading photos containing personal or sensitive information. This feature requires an active internet connection.

Book Calendar & New Release Notifications

When Pro members use the book calendar and new release notifications, the app sends titles of collected works, ISBNs of some volumes, and author names you choose to follow, along with login credentials, to our service to match new books and total volumes against the public catalog. Our service logs daily query counts per account and briefly caches catalog query results that do not contain account identifiers. Purchase history, such as purchase dates and prices, remains strictly on your local device and in your personal backups and is not transmitted as part of this feature.

Feedback & Account Deletion

Feedback text, optional name and contact details, version info, attachments, and video links you submit are stored via Google services for developer troubleshooting. The website BookBuddy converter processes files locally in your browser. To request deletion of your account and personal data, please see the Account Deletion page; Pro subscriptions must be canceled separately through your original app store.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically to comply with legal regulations or service changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.
