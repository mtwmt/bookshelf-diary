See what's new and improved in each version.

  1. v1.6.32026-11-24Current

    Multi-Platform E-book Logging

    • Log the same e-book separately on different platforms.
    • Fixed a crash on launch on iOS 17.
    • Unified button, menu, and form styles for a more consistent experience.
    • Bug fixes and improvements to existing features.
  2. v1.6.22026-11-20

    Japanese & Korean UI and E-book Add Support

    • Added Japanese and Korean language interfaces.
    • Choose Print or E-book in Add Manually and Batch Add.
    • Split Purchases and share images into Print and E-book sections.
    • Share an entire series of works from the series page (Pro).
    • Scan and look up on the fly in Batch Add, and delete mis-scanned books directly (Pro).
    • Tap once to jump to the top or bottom when reordering, with smoother drag-and-drop.
    • Bug fixes and improvements to existing features.
  3. v1.6.02026-11-14

    Records, Loans & E-book Collection

    • Added Records (Pro): leave ratings and notes for books not in your library.
    • Added Loans (Pro): track lent and borrowed books, and keep records after return.
    • Added e-book collection support: track platforms with separate Print and E-book counts.
    • Updated the bottom Add menu; Pro can add from Wishlist to Library and share purchase records.
    • Automatically save a copy of captured covers to your photo album.
  4. v1.5.02026-10-24

    Book Calendar, BookBuddy Import & Improvements

    • Added Book Calendar and new release notifications for Pro to follow authors and works.
    • Import files exported from BookBuddy directly.
    • Bug fixes and improvements: follow system language, sale photo backups, and more stable scanning, search, and sales.
  5. v1.4.02026-10-05

    Pro Subscription & Stability Improvements

    • Pro subscription is now available.
    • Improved stability for sign-in, membership status, and backup/restore.
  6. v1.3.82026-09-26

    Backup, Purchase & Usability Improvements

    • Google Drive backup now supports resuming after interruptions with clearer progress; improved large local backups on Android.
    • Purchase amounts now support multiple currencies and two decimal places with consistent display across screens.
    • Improved input modals, keyboard handling, and account membership stability.
  7. v1.3.72026-09-13

    Purchase Amounts, Categories & Library Organization

    • Added purchase amounts and collection spending summary.
    • Expanded book categories with custom options and reordering.
    • Merge duplicate series or works directly from search results.
    • Added Missing first sorting.
  8. v1.3.62026-08-13

    Bug Fixes & Stability Improvements

    • Fixed known issues and improved overall stability.
  9. v1.3.52026-08-03

    Work Ratings, Reading Status Filter & Cover Crop Improvements

    • Work ratings: rate each work 1 to 5 stars, visible on shelf cards and work pages.
    • Reading status filter: added a filter row in Library with multi-select for Unread, Reading, Read, and Dropped.
    • Cover photo improvements: captured book covers are automatically aligned and straightened.
    • Layout polish: tightened spacing between page headers and content, and unified top list hints (swipeable lists show "Hold to select, swipe for more").
  10. v1.3.42026-07-15

    Excel, Cloud Backup & Scanning Upgrades

    • Excel export/import upgrade: exports include book IDs so re-imports match existing books without creating duplicates when renamed.
    • Google Drive backup upgrade: back up books and covers to your own cloud; Apple Sign-In users can also link Google Drive.
    • Smarter scanning: choose different editions for owned books, and merge manually added books when you scan their barcode later.
    • Box sets and omnibuses now count actual volume counts toward progress, with more accurate duplicate and volume detection.
    • Feedback now supports screenshots, photos, and short videos.
    • Improved cover saving, scan stability, and Wishlist data prefill.
  11. v1.3.32026-04-10

    Share Collection & Dark Mode Polish

    • Share Collection: generate a shareable collection card with one tap (Plus).
    • Refined Dark Mode colors for more comfortable reading.
    • Fixed volume number display on the edit page.
    • Fixed sorting issues for certain books.
  12. v1.3.22026-04-06

    Box Set Fixes & Stats Improvements

    • Box set grid fix: individual volumes covered by a box set are shown in selectable grayscale so you can still track loose copies.
    • Automatic work field correction: calibrates total volumes and box sets on load based on box set ranges.
    • Library stats and progress: expands box sets for more accurate counts.
    • Multi-select action bar no longer covers the last row of books.
  13. v1.3.12026-03-23

    Complete Editing Experience Upgrade

    • Quick shortcuts on the edit page: jump directly to series or work settings from a volume page.
    • Instant refresh on save: see updated info immediately when returning to the work page.
    • Edition field during scan: select hardcover, limited edition, and more right when scanning.
    • Two-way cover sync for single-work series: updating the work cover syncs the series cover.
    • Multi-select move fixes: moving multiple volumes or works to another target now works reliably.
    • Multi-language support: switch between Traditional Chinese, English, and Simplified Chinese.
    • Paywall fixes and volume management refactor.
    • Accessibility and performance improvements.
  14. v1.3.02026-03-18

    • Choose shelves while scanning: file books into shelves right after scanning.
    • Move volumes: long-press a volume to split it into a new work or move it to another work.
    • Books view multi-select: long-press to batch add to shelves or batch delete.
    • Books sorting: sort by name, updated time, date added, or series name.
    • Empty shelf shortcut: quick "Go to Library" button when a shelf is empty.
    • Smoother drag-and-drop reordering across five pages.
    • Higher contrast in Dark Mode for comfortable organizing.
    • Major performance optimizations for large collections.
    • Now available on Google Play for Android users!
