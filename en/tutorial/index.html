Quick Start: Add Your First Book

Choose a method to add books to your library.

Scan ISBN Barcode

Tap "Scan" at the bottom and point at a barcode. Once matched, the title, author, and cover from the source are filled in; confirm the details and "Expected classification:" before adding to your library.

Enter ISBN Manually

Type an ISBN-10 or ISBN-13 directly on the Barcode Scan screen.

Add Manually

No ISBN? Tap "Add" at the bottom, choose "Add Manually", and enter the title.

Catalogue records are primarily in Traditional Chinese; if a book isn't found, you can add it manually. ISBN lookup requires an internet connection.
In the "Add" menu, Batch Add, Search online, and Spine AI Recognition are Pro features.
After saving a book, tap "Continue Scanning"; continuous scanning with batch processing and batch ISBN entry are Pro features.

Four Entry Modes: Catalog Faster

Switch modes at the top of the preview screen based on the book format.

Single / OmnibusMost Common

Add one book and enter its volume number.

Full SeriesAdd Manually Only

Enter total volumes to create volumes 1 through N at once.

One-shot

For standalone books; volume number is set automatically.

Box Set

For box sets, omnibuses, and bundles. Enter the box number and range (e.g., 01-07) for box sets, or connect volumes with + (e.g., 7+8+9) for omnibuses.

The app automatically detects the mode from the title. If you switch modes manually, it won't auto-switch back.

Three-Tier Structure: Series, Work, Volume

The app groups books automatically when scanning, so manual sorting is rarely needed.

Series

The overarching IP. For example, "Naruto" includes the main manga, spin-offs, and movie novels.

Work

Different editions or branches under a series. You can set publication status and category.

Volume

An individual book. Tracks volume number, edition, cover, copies owned, and purchase info.

On the confirmation screen before adding, "Expected classification:" shows the detected grouping. You can adjust it under "Series & Work" if needed.

Collection Progress

The progress card on a work's detail page shows "Owned / Total Volumes" and a progress bar that turns green when complete.

Missing Volumes

Once total volumes are set, missing volumes are listed automatically, e.g., "Missing: 5, 12, 13".

Multiple Sets

Use +/- to adjust "Sets Owned" and see which volumes are missing for your next complete set.

Quick Toggle Owned

Tap volume cells on the work edit page to toggle owned status, or use Select All / Select None at the top right.

Theatrical Works

Movie novelizations are often split into parts; the app provides a dedicated view for them.

Auto Detection: Enabled only when the work title and volume data match chapter grouping criteria; keywords alone are not enough.
Grouped View: View volumes and progress grouped by movie or arc.
Per-Movie Sets: Each movie has its own +/- set counter.
Collapsible: Tap a movie header to collapse or expand it.

Covers & Editing

Custom Cover: Tap the cover on the preview or edit screen to pick from your photo library or take a photo.
Cover Comparison: When a newer online cover is detected on the volume edit screen, compare it with your local cover before choosing.
Contribute Covers: For books missing a cover in the public catalogue, you can choose to share the photo you pick from your album.
Series Cover: Tap the cover on a series detail page to pick from your photo library or child works.

Filter, Sort & Search

Three Filter Rows

Series and Books views in "Library" have three filter rows: Category, Publication Status, and Reading Status. Shelves view has no filter bar.

Multi-Select & Clear

Select multiple chips per row (OR within a row, AND across rows). Tap "All" at the start of a row to clear it.

Search

Tap the search icon at the top right to search series names and aliases in real time.

Customize Categories

If the filter bar is too long, go to "Settings → Library & Purchases → Book Categories", uncheck unused categories, long-press the handle to reorder, and save. Options when adding books update too. Books in hidden categories remain and appear under "Unlisted" when filtering.

Sort Options

✦Default (custom priority)— Open "Reorder" from the sort menu to drag and arrange; new books appear first
✦Favorites— Favorited series appear first
✦Missing first— Works with the most missing volumes appear first
✦Recently updated— Most recently modified items appear first
✦Name A-Z— Sort alphabetically
✦Owned count— Most owned copies appear first

Custom Shelves: Your Own Rules

Group books like real-world shelves and switch views anytime.

Create a Shelf

Switch to "Shelves" in "Library", create a shelf, and name it (e.g., "Favorite Shonen", "To Complete").

Place on Shelf

Assign a work to one or more shelves from its work page.

Shelf View

Select a shelf in the "Shelves" view to browse the works inside.

Edit & Delete

Swipe left on a shelf in Manage Shelves to edit its name/color or delete it; long-press to batch delete.

Sort & Pin

Tap ↕ at the top right to drag and reorder shelves, or swipe left and tap "Pin" to keep one at the top.

Wishlist: Your Bookstore Companion

Keep a list so you never buy duplicates or miss a volume at the bookstore.

Add Manually

Enter a book title to add it to your wishlist.

Scan to Add

Scan a barcode to add; the app alerts you if it's already in your library.

Smart Duplicate Check

When adding from Wishlist to Library, if the same ISBN is already owned, choose to add a copy, add as a different edition, or cancel.

Swipe to Remove

Swipe left to remove an item once bought or no longer wanted.

Batch Add to Library (Pro)

Multi-select items in your Wishlist and add them straight to your library.

Records: Books You've Read Outside Your Library (Pro)

Keep track of library borrows and books you don't own. Tap "Records" in the top menu of "Library".

One Entry per Book

Enter title, author, publisher, plus rating, reading status, notes, and cover.

Want to Collect Later?

You can "Add to Library" or "Add to Wishlist" anytime.

Loans: Lent & Borrowed Books (Pro)

Track who has your books and who you borrowed from. Tap "Loans" in the top menu of "Library".

Lent

Record who you lent it to, the lent date, and an optional return-by date.If you own multiple copies, you can lend part of them; a box set counts as one loan. Even books not in your library can be logged.

Borrowed

Record title, author, publisher, where you borrowed it from, and the loan period, plus reading status and notes.Borrowed books are not added to your library.

Return

Loan history is kept after returning. Lending does not change your owned counts or stats.

E-books cannot be lent.

E-book Collection: Track Print & Digital Together

When adding or editing a volume, set the edition to "E-book".

Platform

Choose Kobo, BookWalker, Readmoo, Google Play, Pubu, or add a custom platform.

Counts & Spending

Home counts show Print and E-book separately; spending can also be split. Set counts include Print only.

How to Add

Barcode scanning defaults to Print; for e-books, enter the eISBN manually.

E-books cannot be sold or lent.

Backup & Restore: Keep a Copy of Your Library

Back up your collection before switching phones or reinstalling.

Unlimited with Plus / Pro; signed-in Free users get 2 total backup/restore trials and 2 total Excel/BookBuddy import/export trials

Google Drive Backup

Link Google Drive and enable Auto Backup so the app attempts backups in the background.Sign in on a new device and choose a backup to restore; this is not real-time sync.

Backup File (Local)

Save your library data and covers as a backup file to import and restore on another device.

Overwrite Restore

Replaces the current database with the backup; existing data is overwritten.

Merge Restore

Keeps existing data and adds non-duplicate records from the backup; duplicates may be skipped.

Import & Export

Export Excel or import templates under "Settings → Collection List (.xlsx)".Import BookBuddy .xlsx / .csv via "Other formats".

Back up current data before restoring, and verify your backup is complete before switching devices or deleting the app. Cloud backup and restore require internet.

Book Calendar: Releases & Purchases at a Glance

Choose "Calendar" from the top menu of "Library" to view new releases and purchase records.

Pro feature; Free / Plus users are directed to the subscription page

Releases

Browse upcoming and recent releases for owned works, same series, and followed authors by publication date, and add them to Wishlist or Library.

New Book Notifications

Tap the notification badge next to "Calendar" to view unseen releases from the last 90 days that aren't in your library or wishlist.When a new volume is found, total volumes and missing volume hints update automatically.

Follow Authors

Tap "+" next to an author's name to follow new releases; works you already own don't need separate following.

Purchases

Once purchase dates are recorded, view monthly purchase counts and recorded amounts.Check "Purchased this month" for the full list, or fill in "Price unrecorded" items.Share a purchase card for a day or the whole month.

Requires internet; the public catalogue may not cover every release on the market. Books without a purchase date are excluded from Purchases.

AI Spine Scan: Add Multiple Books at Once

Photograph book spines, match titles, and add multiple books in one go.

Pro feature; 100 scans per month, requires internet

1. Choose a Photo

Tap "Add" at the bottom, choose "Spine AI Recognition", and take or select a photo.Confirm the crop and tap "Start Recognition"; successful scans use 1 quota (failed scans or zero books detected are refunded).

2. Pick Candidates

Matched books you don't own are listed under "To add"; owned books show "In library".When you see "N possible · tap to choose", select the right book and confirm.

3. Verify Matches

Check items marked "May be wrong · tap to check".For unmatched books, search manually or tap "Scan barcode for ISBN / eISBN" on the confirmation screen.

4. Select & Add to Library

Check the books you want and tap "Add to Library (N)".Once saved to "Scan History", you can return anytime to finish adding; completed sessions are cleaned up after 30 days.

If recognition succeeds but matching fails, stay on the screen and tap "Retry match" without using another scan quota. Failed matches are not saved to Scan History.

Secondhand Sales: Organize & Share Listings

Manage books for sale, share listing text, and record sales manually.

Pro feature

1. Create a Listing

Choose "Sales" from the top menu of "Library", then tap "+" on the For Sale page.Set listings are priced as a whole bundle (not split); individual volumes can be selected and set to allow or disallow splitting.

2. Add Details & Photos

Fill in price, condition, shipping, and actual photos; for individual volumes, you can add per-volume condition and photos.

3. Share Listing

Share text, images, or both to post in groups or marketplaces.Editing fields regenerates the listing text; if a share sheet drops the text, copy the fallback text instead.

4. Record Sale

Tap "Mark as sold" (sold price is optional). Confirming deducts the sold quantity and marks the last copy as not owned.View details in the "Sold" list and "Sale history" on the work page; mistakes can use "Correct as not sold" if eligible.

Actual photos are stored locally and included in current .bookshelf backups. Deleting a listing removes sale settings, text, and photos without deleting your library books.
